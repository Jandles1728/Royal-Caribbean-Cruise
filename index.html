How to lock in the best price

Reprice window closes
—

Final payment for 5+ night sailings is due 90 days out, around Sep 5, 2027. Before that you can ask Royal Caribbean (or your agent) to reprice if the fare drops. They won't do it for you.

Book refundable, then watch

A refundable deposit keeps cancel-and-rebook open if a new promo is marked "new bookings only," which can't be applied as a reprice.

Compare like for like

Check whether a quote includes taxes and port fees, and count perks like onboard credit or BOGO promos. A slightly higher fare with $200 credit can beat a lower bare fare.
